# M04 Server Economy Rules

## Muc dich

Dat ra luat bat di bat dich de bao ve fairness va gia tri lau dai cua economy.

## Hard Rules

- Khong pay to win.
- Khong donate de mua progression power.
- Khong admin grant currency tuy y tru truong hop bu compensation da log.
- Khong drop premium reward tu mini game may man don le.
- Khong de 1 command co ROI vuot qua tat ca command khac.

## Operational Rules

- Moi reward lon phai co logging.
- Moi cap premium currency phai audit duoc.
- Moi event phai co muc tran reward.
- Moi exploit phai co ke hoach dong bang va rollback neu can.

## Catch-up Rules

De nguoi moi khong bi bo lai:

- co onboarding boost
- co weekly target de kiem tai nguyen tot
- co seasonal refresh
- co content khong phu thuoc vao tai san qua cu

## Reset Philosophy

Khong reset trang tay.

Neu can refresh economy, uu tien:

- them sink moi
- seasonal token
- rotating shop
- prestige layer
- content tier moi

## Admin Ethics

Admin la nguoi van hanh, khong la "nguon loot dac biet". Moi dac quyen cap phat can duoc gioi han va co dau vet.
