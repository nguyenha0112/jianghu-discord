# M14 Alchemy

## Fantasy

Nghe chuyen hoa tai nguyen thanh thuoc, buff, catalyst va item utility.

## Role in Economy

Alchemy co the vua la profession vua la transformation system.

No tao ra:

- consumable
- catalyst cho craft cap cao
- item phu tro profession
- event potion / luck boost neu can

## Design Goals

- tao sink cho gathering
- tao lop gia tri gia cong
- khong duoc tro thanh pay-to-win thong qua consumable qua manh

## Balancing Direction

Alchemy nen rat huu dung nhung bi gioi han cong dung, thoi luong hoac tan suat de tranh power creep.
