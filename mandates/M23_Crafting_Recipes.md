# M23 Crafting Recipes

## Muc dich

Bien tai nguyen thanh gia tri cao hon va tao dinh huong cho nhu cau economy.

## Recipe Structure

Moi recipe can co:

- inputs
- station / requirement
- profession level requirement
- success rule
- output
- Xu fee neu co

## Design Rules

- recipe basic de hieu
- recipe cap cao can nhieu lop nguyen lieu
- co mot so recipe mang tinh collection / prestige
- recipe khong chi sinh ra mon do ban shop, ma phai sinh ra item thuc su co gia tri cho player
