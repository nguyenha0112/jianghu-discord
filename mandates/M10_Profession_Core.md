# M10 Profession Core

## Muc dich

Dinh nghia he thong nghe nghiep lam xương song cho progression hang ngay.

## Role of Professions

Nghe nghiep tao ra:

- identity ca nhan
- loop quay lai moi ngay
- nguon material chinh
- ly do nang cap tool
- backbone cho crafting va economy

## Profession Structure

Moi nghe can co:

- level rieng
- action co cooldown hoac energy
- pool material rieng
- tool progression
- drop hiem dac trung
- recipe / exchange lien quan

## Global Rules

- Moi player co the tiep can nhieu nghe, nhung nen co he so uu tien / mastery.
- Nghe de vao, kho de master.
- Moi nghe phai co duong tang truong 30 ngay va 90 ngay.

## Current Profession Set

- Fishing
- Mining
- Gathering
- Alchemy
- Archaeology

## Cross-System Role

- Profession cap material cho item system.
- Profession cap demand cho tool va consumable.
- Profession tao aspiration qua item hiem va discovery.
