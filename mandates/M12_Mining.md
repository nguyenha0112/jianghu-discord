# M12 Mining

## Fantasy

Dao quang, tim kim loai va tai nguyen khoang vat de che tao vat pham gia tri cao.

## Core Outputs

- ore tiers
- gem chance
- stone / crystal
- rare metal

## Progression

- pickaxe tiers
- unlock vein tiers
- deeper node = gia tri cao hon

## Design Goals

- tao backbone cho crafting kinh te
- la nghe co utility rat cao
- co kha nang san sinh rare material quan trong

## Balancing Direction

Mining nen co effort cao hon fishing, reward cao hon trung binh, la nghe quan trong cho item progression.
