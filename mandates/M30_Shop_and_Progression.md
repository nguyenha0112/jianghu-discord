# M30 Shop and Progression

## Muc dich

Shop khong chi la noi tieu tien ma la cong cu dinh huong progression.

## Shop Layers

- basic shop
- profession shop
- cosmetic shop
- rotating shop
- event shop

## Design Rules

- vat pham trong shop phai mo ra muc tieu moi
- khong ban thang phan thuong endgame qua re
- rotating inventory giup tao FOMO nhe, khong doc hai

## Progression Role

Player vao shop de:

- mua tool
- mua ingredient con thieu
- tieu Xu vao muc tieu ro rang
- tieu Ngoc vao aspiration reward
