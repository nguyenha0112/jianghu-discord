# M03 Rarity and Value

## Muc dich

Tao ngon ngu gia tri chung cho vat pham, phan thuong va progression.

## Rarity Tiers

1. `Common`
2. `Uncommon`
3. `Rare`
4. `Epic`
5. `Legendary`
6. `Mythic` hoac `Relic` neu can cho endgame

## Y nghia cua rarity

Rarity khong chi la mau ten. No anh huong den:

- do hiem cua drop
- do kho cua recipe
- gia shop / gia trao doi
- muc do khoe / dep / sưu tam
- muc do bat buoc trong progression

## Value Model

Gia tri cua item duoc xac dinh boi:

- do hiem
- utility
- effort de tao ra
- so luong source
- nhu cau cua system khac
- tinh suu tam / cosmetic

## Design Rules

- Rare khong dong nghia voi bat buoc manh hon.
- Cosmetic item van co the hiem ma khong gay power creep.
- Material hiem phai co dung cho dac biet, khong duoc de "hiem cho dep".
- Rarity cao phai co cau chuyen nguon goc: boss, event, collection, deep profession, long recipe.

## Healthy Distribution

- Common: fuel cho loop hang ngay
- Uncommon: muc tang truong de thay tien bo
- Rare: muc tieu ngan han dang mo uoc
- Epic: thanh tuu ro rang
- Legendary+: muc tieu dai han / prestige
