# M11 Fishing

## Fantasy

Nguoi choi cam thay minh dang cau ca, san loot nuoc va tim do hiem tu song ho.

## Core Outputs

- fish common
- fish rare
- shell / pearl / water herb
- junk item co the refine
- chance drop relic nuoc

## Progression

- rod basic -> improved -> elite
- bait tiers
- fishing level unlock zone / loot pool

## Design Goals

- de tiep can cho nguoi moi
- reward deu, de tao cam giac on dinh
- co xac suat cao hon cho collection item nuoc

## Risks

- qua on dinh se thanh best default grind
- qua random se gay uc che

## Balancing Direction

Fishing nen la nghe on dinh, thu nhap trung binh, collection tot, co co hoi gap do hiem nho.
