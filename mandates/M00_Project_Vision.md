# M00 Project Vision

## Muc dich

Tai lieu nay khoa dinh danh tinh cua project `Jianghu Discord`: day la mot community game tren Discord co progression lau dai, khong phai mot economy bot ngan han.

## Design Intent

- Tao cam giac bang hoi MMORPG.
- Khuyen khich quay lai moi ngay ma khong bat buoc online lau.
- Bien moi thanh vien thanh mot "nhan vat" co qua trinh lon len.
- Duy tri gia tri tu tien do, collection va danh hieu.

## Success Criteria

- New user hieu loop co ban trong 3 phut.
- Sau 7 ngay, user van con muc tieu tiep theo.
- Sau 30 ngay, user co dau an ca nhan: nghe, vat pham, cosmetic, title, hoac collection.
- Community co chu de de noi chuyen, khoe do, trao doi va tranh tai.

## Target Experience

Nguoi choi phai thay:

- "Minh dang xay dung mot nhan vat."
- "Moc tiep theo rat ro rang."
- "Neu cham chi, minh se mo khoa duoc nhieu thu."
- "Khong can nap tien de cam thay co gia tri."

## Non-Goals

- Khong bien server thanh casino.
- Khong dua core fun vao giveaway ngau nhien.
- Khong de progression bi chi phoi boi donate.
- Khong lam he thong phuc tap den muc can doc huong dan qua dai moi choi duoc.

## Product Positioning

`Jianghu Discord` nam giua ba the loai:

- community server
- light MMORPG
- live operated progression game

No khac voi economy bot thong thuong o cho gia tri den tu he thong loop va identity, khong den tu viec spam lenh.

## Strategic Principle

Moi tinh nang moi phai tra loi duoc 3 cau hoi:

1. No lam ro hon danh tinh MMORPG hay khong?
2. No tang long-term progression hay chi tao dopamine ngan han?
3. No co giu duoc can bang va fairness hay khong?
